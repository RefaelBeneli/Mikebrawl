# Audio walkthrough (free)

Get free music and sounds for the game, step by step. Every file is optional: anything you skip keeps the game's built-in sound, so you can do a few at a time.

You need: a free **Pixabay** account (music and sound effects, free for games, no credit needed). About 1 hour.

---

## Step 1: Set up a folder

1. Create a folder: `~/Downloads/audio`.
2. Save everything you download there.
3. Rename each file to the name shown below (e.g. `level1.mp3`). The names matter: the game finds sounds by name.

---

## Step 2: Music (7 files) from Pixabay Music

### How to get one track

1. Open <https://pixabay.com/music/> and sign in.
2. Type the search words for the track (below) into the search box.
3. Press play on a few results. Pick one that fits.
4. Press **Download** (MP3) and rename it.

Tips:
- Look for tracks tagged **8-bit**, **chiptune**, **retro game**, **arcade**, or **synthwave**. They fit the game best.
- Skip tracks with singing.
- Use the **duration** filter: songs 1-3 minutes, jingles under 15 seconds.

| Save as | Plays during | Search words | Pick something that feels... |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title.mp3` | title and gym screens | `retro game theme`, `8-bit hero` | confident, heroic, catchy |
| `level1.mp3` | Level 1, Hadar at sunset | `retro arcade funky`, `chiptune upbeat` | upbeat, funky, sunny |
| `level2.mp3` | Level 2, Upper Carmel at night | `synthwave night`, `retro chill game` | cool, smooth, night city |
| `level3.mp3` | Level 3, Romema, almost home | `chiptune action`, `retro game final level` | fast, tense, determined |
| `boss.mp3` | every boss fight | `boss battle 8-bit`, `retro boss fight` | intense, dangerous, fast |
| `victory.mp3` | level clear | `victory jingle 8-bit`, `level complete` | short and triumphant (under 10 s) |
| `gameover.mp3` | K.O. screen | `game over 8-bit`, `retro game over` | short and sad (under 10 s) |

The game loops the whole music file, so a track that ends cleanly loops best.

## Step 3: Sound effects (40 files) from Pixabay Sound Effects

### How to get one sound

1. Open <https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/>.
2. Search the words below. Play a few, pick the best.
3. Prefer **short** sounds (under 1 second) that start right away, with no silence at the start.
4. Download (MP3) and rename.

Can't find a good one? Skip it. The built-in sound stays.

## Other free sources

- **Freesound** (<https://freesound.org>): huge sound library. Use the license filter **Creative Commons 0** (no credit needed).
- **OpenGameArt** (<https://opengameart.org>): free game music. Pick files marked **CC0**. Other licenses need a credit line; tell me and I'll add it to the game.
- **ElevenLabs** free plan: makes custom voices and effects from text, a limited amount each month.
