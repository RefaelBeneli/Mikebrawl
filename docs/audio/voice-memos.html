Record the voices

About 15 minutes. You record one clip per character, with a short pause between sounds. I cut each clip into separate sounds and add them to the game.

Before you start: free sound packs

For hits and menu sounds, download these two zips (free, no account, no credit needed) and put them in ~/Downloads/audio:

  • Kenney Impact Sounds
  • Kenney Interface Sounds

On each page, tap Download. If it asks for a donation, you can skip it: the packs are free.

Step 1: Set up

  1. Find a quiet room: no fan, no TV, door closed.
  2. Open your phone's recorder app: Voice Recorder on Samsung, Recorder on Pixel. Any recorder app works.
  3. Samsung: tap ⋮ → Settings → Recording quality → High. Pixel: nothing to set.
  4. Hold the phone about a hand's length (20 cm) from your mouth, microphone (bottom of the phone) facing you.
  5. Shouts: turn your face a little to the side, so they don't distort.

Step 2: How to record each clip

  1. Tap the record button.
  2. Make the sounds in the exact order on the card.
  3. After each sound, stay silent for 2 seconds (count "one, two" in your head).
  4. Tap stop and save it with the name on the card (e.g. mike).

No words. Grunts, shouts and laughs only. Overdo it: game voices sound best when they are big and short.

mikeMike: your normal voice, tough and confident
  1. Punch: short sharp "hah!"
  2. Punch: another "hah!", a little different
  3. Kick: longer "hyaa!"
  4. Kick: another "hyaa!"
  5. Hit: short pained "ugh!"
  6. Hit: another "oof!"
  7. Knocked down: long falling groan "aaahh…"
thugThug: big, slow, deep voice (push your voice low)
  1. Taunt: mocking low laugh "heh heh heh"
  2. Hit: short low grunt "hrgh!"
  3. Hit: another low grunt
  4. Knocked out: long low groan
runnerRunner: skinny, fast, higher voice
  1. Taunt: cheeky two-note whistle (up, then down)
  2. Hit: short high yelp "yip!"
  3. Hit: another yelp
  4. Knocked out: long falling cry "waaah…"
big_tonyBig Tony (boss): huge, very deep, slow
  1. Intro: slow threatening growl "hrrrmmm"
  2. Hit: short deep grunt
  3. Angry: roar that gets louder "rrraaAAH!"
  4. Knocked out: long heavy groan
viperViper (boss): fast, sly, sharp voice
  1. Intro: hissing snicker "sss-heh-heh"
  2. Hit: short sharp grunt "tch!"
  3. Angry: hiss that turns into a shout "ssssHAH!"
  4. Knocked out: long cry
the_chiefThe Chief (final boss): older, raspy, in charge
  1. Intro: cold low laugh "hm-hm-hm"
  2. Hit: short raspy grunt
  3. Angry: furious roar that rises
  4. Knocked out: long raspy groan
peoplePeople on the street (ask a friend for the scream, or skip it)
  1. Woman: short startled scream
  2. Old man: annoyed grumble "hmph, mmh-mm"
  3. Tired man: quiet sleepy mutter "mmh… mm…"

Any card you skip keeps its built-in sound. Cat and rat sounds stay built-in.

Step 3: Send them to the Mac

  1. In the recorder app, select all the clips → Share → Drive (Google Drive) → upload.
  2. On the Mac, open drive.google.com, select the clips → Download.
  3. Move the files (or the zip Drive makes) into ~/Downloads/audio.
  4. Tell me. I cut, name, set the volume, and you listen before it goes live.

Quick check before sending

  • Each clip has the sounds in the card order.
  • There's a clear 2-second silence between sounds.
  • Nothing sounds crackly (if it does, record again a bit further away).
  • File names match the cards: mike, thug, runner…
